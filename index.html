Our Products

What we supply

Green Cardamom Flagship

Elettaria cardamomum · HS 0908 31

Premium Alleppey green cardamom from Kerala — deep green, bold pods, high oil content.

  • AGEB (8mm+), AGB (7–8mm), AGS (6–7mm)
  • Moisture ≤12% · Volatile oil 6–8%
  • Bulk & vacuum packing to your spec

Black Pepper

Piper nigrum · HS 0904

Whole black pepper from South India — clean, aromatic, graded to international standards.

  • Garbled & ungarbled grades
  • 500 G/L & 550 G/L bulk density
  • FCL / LCL from Kochi

Coconut Products

Kerala origin

Coconut products from India's coconut heartland, for food manufacturers and distributors.

  • Desiccated coconut & more
  • Food-grade packing
  • Volume supply on schedule
